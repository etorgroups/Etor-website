ETOR City

One managed place, not a scattered land bank.

ETOR City spans roughly 250 acres and is still extending, laid out across four locations along the Andhra Pradesh coast: Sariapalli, Sottadivalasa and Ichapuram. Each plot sits inside one managed development combining plantation plots, hospitality and on-ground amenities — not just a title deed on an empty field.
