# ETOR Group — Phase 1 Creative Brief

Status: working direction extracted from `etorBroucher.docx` on 2026-08-08

## Creative premise

**ETOR Group turns land into a living, long-term asset.**

The experience should move from the physical world (soil, water, orchards, dairy and
community) to the human outcome (security, belonging and participation in growth).
It should feel like a premium land-and-lifestyle group with an investment layer—not a
generic financial dashboard and not a collection of unrelated venture cards.

## Source truth extracted from the brochure

- ETOR City and ETOR Ventures are the primary brand names shown.
- The brochure presents ETOR City 1 and 2 at ₹3,999 per sq. yd and ETOR City 3 & 4 at
  ₹4,999 per sq. yd.
- Locations shown: Sariapalle, Sottadivalasa and Ichapuram.
- The programme includes river-view plots, organic dairy, Miyazaki mango, sandalwood,
  sitafal and dragon fruit cultivation.
- Described amenities include accommodation, restaurant, OTT theatre, security,
  CCTV, landscaped gardens, power/water facilities, swimming pool, cycling, horse
  riding, maintenance and clear-title claims.
- The brochure describes a cashback / return model, including 100% cashback in 100
  months and additional crop-related return examples.
- The brochure lists an ETOR Ventures address in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh and
  a 2014 founding reference appears in the existing project data.

## Claims requiring verification before publication

The frontend may present brochure claims as “programme terms” or “as described in the
brochure” until the owner verifies them legally and commercially:

- guaranteed / 100% cashback language;
- return projections, crop prices and income tables;
- “world’s most expensive fruit” language;
- “first in India” language;
- certification, title, maintenance duration and amenity availability;
- Google review ratings and any customer testimonial.

## Story arc

1. **The ground** — a cinematic, calm opening on land, water and cultivation.
2. **The proposition** — ETOR creates a managed ecosystem around that land.
3. **The living portfolio** — cities, orchards, dairy and high-value crops are shown as
   connected parts of one system.
4. **The participation model** — packages, plot details and stated programme terms are
   explained in plain language with a visible disclaimer.
5. **The proof** — development, amenities, documentation, people and contact details.
6. **The invitation** — a quiet, confident conversation CTA rather than pressure.

## Experience direction

- One focal message per viewport.
- Editorial typography: a high-contrast serif for statements and a practical sans for
  details.
- Dark mineral surfaces, porcelain text, muted bronze actions and botanical green as a
  supporting signal.
- Real imagery receives a deliberate scrim before text; no text depends on a busy photo
  for contrast.
- Motion is slow and purposeful: camera-like reveals, gentle parallax and transitions
  that explain relationships. No perpetual decorative motion.
- The website must feel tactile and quiet: fewer cards, fewer badges, more breathing room,
  stronger alignment and clearer proof.

## Homepage scene map

1. Hero: “Build value that keeps growing.”
2. Proof rail: years, locations, land scale and programme documents.
3. ETOR City: a single flagship landscape story.
4. Living assets: orchard, dairy, sandalwood, sitafal and dragon fruit.
5. How participation works: a transparent five-step journey.
6. Packages: compare the three city options without hype.
7. Development and amenities: tangible proof, not abstract claims.
8. Questions and disclosures.
9. Contact: speak to an advisor / request the master plan.

## Content rules

- Preserve the brochure’s meaning but rewrite unclear wording into plain, correct English.
- Never invent financial performance, certifications, locations or testimonials.
- Place the terms/disclaimer adjacent to any return, cashback or price claim.
- Every image has a descriptive alt text; decorative images are explicitly marked as such.
- Every animation has a static, readable state for reduced-motion users.
